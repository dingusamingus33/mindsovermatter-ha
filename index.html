01 / Positioning

Clear thinking for complex healthcare decisions.

Healthcare is never just a market. It is a system of unique people with unique opportunities, workflows, constraints, and outcomes. We bring a disciplined, practical, and deeply human perspective to healthcare services strategy, helping organizations understand what matters, where value lives, and how to move with confidence without drifting from their mission.

02 / Services

Across the healthcare services lifecycle.

Four practice areas, applied independently or together, sized to the decision, not the deck.

01

Diligence

Assess healthcare services opportunities with a clear view of the market, operating realities, patient needs, growth potential, risks, and value levers.
02

Strategy

Translate complexity into focused strategic direction — from market entry and growth planning to service line strategy, competitive positioning, and operating priorities.
03

Advisory

Support leaders, investors, and teams with practical guidance rooted in healthcare experience, sound judgment, and an understanding of what it takes to execute.
04

Experience & Outcomes

Evaluate how care delivery, access, engagement, workflows, and systems shape experience — and how better design can improve outcomes across the continuum of care.
03 / Why Minds Over Matter

Healthcare is a systems problem. It is also a human one.

The best healthcare decisions balance evidence, economics, operations, and empathy. Minds Over Matter is built on the belief that durable healthcare value comes from aligning business strategy with patient needs, provider realities, and measurable outcomes.

iPatient-centered perspective
iiOperator-aware strategy
iiiDiligence grounded in real-world healthcare dynamics
ivFocus on outcomes, experience, and sustainable growth
vClear recommendations, not unnecessary complexity
04 / Fit

The right partner is a fit — not a pitch.

Many advisors talk a good game. The quieter, more important question is whether the working relationship will hold up when the stakes are real. Fit is what allows trust to form, candor to flow, and good decisions to follow.

Trust, earned in the work
Built through candor, follow-through, and the willingness to say what is hard.
Fit before fee
Engagements only begin when both sides believe the work is the right work, together.
Lived experience
The kind of understanding that only comes from being on every side of care — as clinicians, as caregivers, as patients walking through a diagnosis of our own.
05 / Approach

Ask. Think. Be Human.

A simple working method for complex problems designed to deliver judgment, not deliverables for their own sake. We are not perfect, but we will get close enough together.

Step 01

Understand

Start with the full picture: market, model, stakeholders, incentives, risks, and the patient journey.

Step 02

Focus

Separate signal from noise. Identify the few decisions that matter most.

Step 03

Advise

Deliver clear, actionable guidance that helps teams move forward with confidence.

06 / About

A thoughtful advisory partner for healthcare services.

Minds Over Matter reflects our commitment to healthcare, patient and employee experience, outcomes, and the systems that shape care delivery. We bring a strategic and practical lens to healthcare services work grounded in heart, helping clients navigate complexity with clarity, rigor, and purpose, without losing sight of what's important.

Healthcare deserves advisors who take both the business and the bedside seriously. That belief shapes every engagement.

We are culture-first founders with experience on all sides of the equation — clinicians, operators, providers, architects, and builders who have also been caregivers, patients, and family members navigating our own challenges. Some things in this work cannot be learned in a classroom — only lived.
